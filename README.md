# Awesome Household Apps [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of apps for running a shared home: splitting bills, shopping
> lists, chores, notes, plants and events — for couples, families and
> flatmates.

Apps are listed alphabetically within each section. 🔓 marks open-source
projects.

## All-in-one Household Organizers

- [Cozi](https://www.cozi.com) - Family organizer with a shared calendar, lists, chores and meal planning.
- [Flatastic](https://www.flatastic-app.com) - Flatshare app with a chores rota, shared shopping list and shared costs.
- [Homsy](https://gethomsy.com) - Offline-first household organizer with rotating chores, pantry barcode scanning, family calendar and utility tracking.
- [Klusek](https://klusek.app) - One calm place for everything you're managing: shared expenses, shopping lists, to-dos, notes, plant care and guest lists, shareable with your household.
- [OurHome](https://ourhomeapp.com) - Chores with rewards for families with kids, plus a shared shopping list.
- [OurMana](https://ourmana.app) - Family organizer built around a shared household chat, with grocery list, meal planner, chores and recipes.
- [SameNest](https://www.samenest.com) - Home management for couples and roommates with bank statement import, shared grocery list and document storage.

## Shared Expenses

- [Honeydue](https://www.honeydue.com) - Finance app for couples with bank sync, bill reminders and a shared budget.
- [Settle Up](https://settleup.io) - Split costs in groups and on trips, with multiple currencies.
- [Splid](https://splid.app) - Split group expenses offline, without creating an account.
- [Spliit](https://spliit.app) - 🔓 Web-first, open-source alternative to Splitwise.
- [Splitwise](https://www.splitwise.com) - The best-known app for tracking shared bills and who owes whom.
- [Tricount](https://www.tricount.com) - Free group expense splitting, popular for trips in Europe.

## Shopping Lists

- [AnyList](https://www.anylist.com) - Shared grocery lists with recipes and meal planning.
- [Bring!](https://www.getbring.com) - Free shared shopping list with item icons, popular in Europe.
- [Listonic](https://listonic.com) - Smart shopping list with sharing and price tracking.
- [OurGroceries](https://www.ourgroceries.com) - Simple shopping list that syncs instantly across the family.
- [Out of Milk](https://www.outofmilk.com) - Shopping list with pantry tracking and to-dos.

## Chores and To-dos

- [Any.do](https://www.any.do) - To-dos, calendar and grocery list with a family plan.
- [Microsoft To Do](https://to-do.office.com) - Free task manager with shared lists.
- [TickTick](https://ticktick.com) - To-dos with calendar, habit tracker and focus timer.
- [Tiimo](https://www.tiimoapp.com) - Visual day planner, popular with neurodivergent people.
- [Tody](https://todyapp.com) - Cleaning planner based on how dirty each area gets, not a fixed schedule.
- [Todoist](https://www.todoist.com) - Polished task manager with shared projects.

## Notes and Checklists

- [Apple Notes](https://www.apple.com/icloud/) - Built-in notes on Apple devices with shared notes and checklists.
- [Google Keep](https://keep.google.com) - Quick notes and checklists with easy sharing.
- [Joplin](https://joplinapp.org) - 🔓 Notes with end-to-end encryption and sync through any cloud.
- [Notion](https://www.notion.com) - Flexible workspace for notes, databases and "life OS" templates.
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) - Local-first knowledge base with a large plugin ecosystem.
- [Simplenote](https://simplenote.com) - 🔓 Minimal, free, synced notes.
- [Standard Notes](https://standardnotes.com) - 🔓 End-to-end encrypted notes.

## Plant Care

- [Greg](https://greg.app) - Plant identification and watering based on the plant and the room, with a community.
- [Pl@ntNet](https://plantnet.org) - Free, citizen-science plant identification.
- [PictureThis](https://www.picturethisai.com) - Plant identification and diagnosis from a photo.
- [Planta](https://getplanta.com) - Care schedules, plant identification and a light meter.

## Events and Guest Lists

- [Evite](https://www.evite.com) - Classic online invitations and RSVPs.
- [Joy](https://withjoy.com) - Free wedding website with guest list and RSVPs.
- [Partiful](https://partiful.com) - Free party invites and RSVPs.
- [RSVPify](https://rsvpify.com) - RSVP and guest management for weddings and events.
- [The Knot](https://www.theknot.com) - Wedding planning platform with guest list and RSVPs.
- [Zola](https://www.zola.com) - Wedding planning with guest list, RSVPs, website and registry.

## Family Screen Time

- [Ripple](https://ripplefocus.github.io/) - Free screen time scheduler for Android and iPhone that blocks short videos and games at set hours, with a parent unlock code and uninstall protection.

## Contributing

Contributions are welcome. Open a pull request that adds the app to the right
section in alphabetical order, using the format
`- [Name](url) - Short description.` Please add only apps that are actively
maintained and useful for a shared home.
